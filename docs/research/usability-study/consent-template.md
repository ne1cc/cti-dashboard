## Plain-Language Participant Information

### What is this study about?
We are developing an open-access website designed to help patients, caregivers, navigators, and clinicians find and understand clinical trials. We want to learn how easy or difficult the website is to use, whether the information presented is clear, and how we can improve it.

### What will I be asked to do?
- You will be asked to complete several realistic trial search tasks using a web browser.
- We will ask you to "think aloud" as you explore the site—sharing what you are looking for, what makes sense, and what feels confusing.
- We will ask a few short comprehension questions about what you see on the screen.
- Total session time is approximately 30 minutes.

### We are testing the website, not you
There are no right or wrong answers. If something is difficult or confusing, it is a flaw in the website's design, not in your ability. Your honest reactions will help us improve the tool.

### No Personal Health Information (Zero-PHI Policy)
Please do **not** tell us about your own medical history, personal diagnoses, treatments, or private healthcare experiences. We provide fictional, practice scenarios for all tasks.

### Is participation voluntary?
Yes. Taking part in this usability study is completely voluntary:
- You may skip any task or refuse to answer any question at any time.
- You can pause or stop the session at any moment without giving a reason.
- Deciding not to participate or withdrawing will carry no penalty or loss of benefits.

### Privacy and Confidentiality
- Your name, email address, phone number, and any identifying details will **never** be published, committed to code repositories, or linked to your feedback.
- Notes and observations are recorded under an anonymous participant code (such as `P1`, `P2`, `P3`, `P4`, or `P5`).
- Summaries of feedback and aggregated findings will be used solely for open-source software improvement.
